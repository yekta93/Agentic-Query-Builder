# Example 002 – Blocked Foreign Connections

**Question**  
Find blocked connections coming from countries other than Iran in the last hour and show a timeseries by event type

**Generated Query**
```slaql
from last 1h
| where action == "blocked" and country != "IR"
| timeseries count() by event_type
```

**Step-by-step reasoning**
1. Time range: last 1 hour
2. Filter action = blocked AND country not equal to IR
3. Produce a timeseries aggregation by event_type
